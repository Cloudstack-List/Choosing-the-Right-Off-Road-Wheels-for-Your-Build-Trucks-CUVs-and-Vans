Choosing the Right Off-Road Wheels for Your Build: Trucks, CUVs, and Vans

Not all wheels work with all vehicles. This comprehensive guide matches our wide range of wheels to CUVs, Trucks, and Vans.

There is a general understanding that different vehicle makes require different wheel solutions. Every vehicle has its own unique flair. Every wheel has its own intended use case. Especially so within the off roading scene, where wheel and vehicle compatibility is completely essential.

The wheel requirements for a CUV are vastly different from those needed on an adventure van. What performs on your truck simply won’t handle the demands of a fully loaded overland rig. This guide explores our curated wheel selection here at REIKA, matching specific designs to trucks, crossovers, and vans based on their unique performance needs and load requirements.

Trucks: Built for the Intensity

Trucks are made for intense travels. Whether you’re towing, hauling gear, or tackling tough trails, your wheels need to hold their own. Load rating matters here, but so does the style and stance.

R35 Rambler

Our R35 Rambler series is a true workhorse featuring lightweight yet durable flow-formed construction with a simulated beadlock style that’s street-legal. A plethora of finishes, the wheel also delivers in the very popular -38 offset for those who want that aggressive stance

That’s not all. A beadlock style was also crafted. The R35B adds true forged beadlock functionality for those dropping tire pressure and heading into rugged terrain. The outer ring design adds strength to the wheel’s outer edge making it more durable, while the knurled bead seats provide improved tire security. Both models feature Machined Clear finishes that not only maintain a bold appearance, but also prevent corrosion with demanding use.

A flow form wheel through and through, the R40 Trooper is manufactured with premium grade materials to assure lightweight performance and strong terrain navigation.

Available in 17×8.5 and 17×9, with many common bolt pattern fitments for trucks in both a +0 and -12 offset.

R45 Marauder

Setting new benchmarks in off-road wheel design, the R45 Marauder series represents our most advanced engineering achievement. Choose the standard R45 for a simulated beadlock aesthetic with street-friendly functionality, or go with the R45B’s true forged beadlock system for rugged terrain capability.

The forged construction withstands the forces of heavy payload and aggressive off roading. Meanwhile the precision-engineered beadlock ring maintains tire security even when running low pressures. Engineered at the highest level, this is where form meets performance.

Fit for every exploration, while maintaining the rugged style for those who wish to off road. Why Fit Matters More Than Flash

Wheel fitment, construction, and load rating matter just as much as style or looks, if not more. Here’s what to keep in mind:

  • Load Rating: Choose wheels that meet or exceed your vehicle’s Gross Axle Weight Rating. You can usually find it on the door jamb sticker. Take the highest GAWR, divide it by two, and ensure your wheels are rated for at least that.
  • Construction: Cast wheels are affordable and durable. Flow-formed wheels offer enhanced strength without excessive weight. Forged wheels are your best bet for extreme use. Construction absolutely matters.
  • Fitment: Size and offset affect everything from tire clearance to suspension. Make sure it works with your lift, tire size, and fenders.
